Privacy Policy

Last updated: Dec 29, 2025

Summary

  • No account. FitTrack does not require sign-in.
  • No server storage. FitTrack runs in your browser and stores data locally on your device.
  • No ads / tracking. FitTrack does not use analytics or tracking cookies.

What data FitTrack stores

FitTrack stores the data you enter, such as:

  • Profile info (name, preferred weight units)
  • Exercises (built-in list plus any custom exercises you create)
  • Workouts (dates, notes, exercises, sets, reps, weights)

Where it is stored

FitTrack stores data in your browser’s local storage (localStorage) on this device. This data stays on your device unless you export it.

Export / Import

You can export your FitTrack data as a JSON file and import it later (for backups or moving to another device). Imported data replaces the data currently stored in your browser.

Cookies

FitTrack does not set tracking cookies. Your browser may still store standard site data as part of normal operation.

Third-party services

FitTrack does not send your fitness data to any third-party service by default.

Data deletion

You can delete your FitTrack data at any time via Settings → Reset all data inside the app, or by clearing your browser storage for this site.
